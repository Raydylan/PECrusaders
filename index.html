Our Story

A Tradition Since 1843

PE Crusader Sports Club has a rich history dating back to 1843, making it one of the oldest sporting clubs in South Africa.

Located at St George's Park in Gqeberha, the club brings together sporting excellence, friendship and community spirit.

With multiple sporting codes and a welcoming clubhouse, Crusaders continues to build on its proud sporting heritage.
